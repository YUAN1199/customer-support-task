# Acceptable Use Policy

## Document ID: POL-002
## Min Role: employee

Effective Date: 2025-01-01

## 1. Overview

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) governs the use of AcmeCorp's information technology resources, including computers, networks, software, email, and internet access. By using any AcmeCorp IT resource, you agree to comply with this policy.

## 2. General Principles

- IT resources are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted if it does not interfere with work duties or consume excessive resources.
- You are responsible for all activity conducted under your account credentials. Never share your password.
- Respect intellectual property rights. Do not install unlicensed software.
- Do not use AcmeCorp resources for any illegal activity.

## 3. Internet Usage

- Web browsing is monitored for security purposes. HTTPS traffic is decrypted at the perimeter for threat inspection.
- Prohibited: accessing pornography, gambling sites, torrent sites, or any content that could create a hostile work environment.
- Downloading executables from untrusted sources is blocked by default. Submit a ticket to IT for exceptions.

## 4. Email Usage

- Corporate email must not be used for harassment, chain letters, or solicitation.
- Be cautious with attachments from unknown senders. Report phishing to phishing@acmecorp.com.
- External email of Tier 3+ data is prohibited unless using approved encryption.

## 5. Software Installation

- Only software from the approved catalog (accessible via the Company Portal) may be installed.
- Open-source software requires legal review for license compatibility.
- Developer tools must be approved by Engineering Security before use.

## 6. Remote Access

- VPN is required for accessing internal resources from outside the office.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all remote access.
- Do not access internal systems from public Wi-Fi without VPN.

## 7. Enforcement

Violations will be investigated by IT Security. Consequences may include:
- Temporary or permanent revocation of access
- Disciplinary action
- Legal action for criminal violations
