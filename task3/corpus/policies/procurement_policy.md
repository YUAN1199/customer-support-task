# Procurement Policy

## Document ID: POL-011
## Min Role: manager

Effective Date: 2025-01-01

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the acquisition of goods and services at AcmeCorp.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

| Amount | Approver Required |
|--------|------------------|
| < $1,000 | Team Lead |
| $1,000 - $10,000 | Manager |
| $10,000 - $50,000 | Director |
| $50,000 - $250,000 | VP |
| > $250,000 | CFO + CEO |

## 3. Vendor Selection

- For purchases over $10,000, obtain at least 3 competitive quotes.
- For software/SaaS, consult IT Security for vendor risk assessment.
- Preferred vendors are listed in the procurement portal.

## 4. Purchase Orders

All purchases over $500 require a PO number issued through Coupa. Invoices without a PO will be rejected.

## 5. Supplier Code of Conduct

All suppliers must comply with AcmeCorp's Supplier Code of Conduct covering labor practices, environmental standards, and ethical business conduct.
