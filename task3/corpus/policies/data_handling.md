# Data Handling Policy

## Document ID: POL-001
## Min Role: employee

Effective Date: 2025-01-01
Last Updated: 2025-06-15

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the framework for handling all forms of data within AcmeCorp, including customer data, employee records, financial information, and intellectual property. All employees are responsible for understanding and following these guidelines.

## 2. Data Classification

Data at AcmeCorp is classified into four tiers:

### Tier 1: Public
Information approved for public release. Examples: press releases, product brochures, job listings. No access restrictions.

### Tier 2: Internal
Information intended for employees only. Examples: internal wiki, team directories, project roadmaps. Accessible to all employees and above.

### Tier 3: Confidential
Sensitive business or personal information. Examples: customer PII, financial reports, legal documents. Access restricted to employees with a business need.

### Tier 4: Restricted
Highly sensitive information requiring special handling. Examples: trade secrets, merger plans, security incident reports. Access limited to managers and above with explicit authorization.

## 3. Data Storage

- Tier 1 and Tier 2 data may be stored on company-approved cloud services (Google Workspace, Confluence, SharePoint).
- Tier 3 data must be stored in encrypted repositories with access logging enabled.
- Tier 4 data must be stored in isolated, encrypted vaults with multi-factor authentication and quarterly access reviews.

## 4. Data Sharing

- Never share Tier 3 or Tier 4 data over unencrypted channels (plain email, Slack DMs without enterprise key management).
- External sharing of any data requires manager approval.
- Use AcmeCorp's secure file transfer system for sharing files larger than 25 MB.

## 5. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Email communications | 3 years |
| Financial records | 7 years |
| Customer PII | 5 years after last interaction |
| HR records | 7 years after employment ends |
| System logs | 1 year |

## 6. Data Disposal

Data must be disposed of securely:
- Digital data: Use DoD 5220.22-M compliant wiping or physical destruction of storage media.
- Physical documents: Cross-cut shredding (DIN 66399 level P-4 or higher).

## 7. Incident Reporting

Any suspected data breach or policy violation must be reported to the Security Operations Center within 1 hour of discovery. Use the incident hotline (extension 5555) or email soc@acmecorp.com (internal only). Do not discuss the incident with unauthorized parties.

## 8. Consequences

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment and legal action.
